Privacy Policy

OR Gmail Reader is a personal desktop application used by its owner only.

  • The app uses the Gmail read-only scope (gmail.readonly) for the owner's own account only.
  • It reads only messages needed for the owner's personal automation.
  • Data is processed locally on the owner's private computer and is not sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.
  • No data is used for advertising or for training AI models.
  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer, and access can be revoked anytime at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

OR Gmail Reader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: caniehaus@gmail.com
